Wi-Fi Radar

Invisible Safety Monitoring Through RF Sensing

Wi-Fi Radar transforms standard wireless signals into an invisible safety monitoring network around your pool. Without any cameras or optical surveillance, the system detects human presence, movement patterns, and even micro-movements like breathing — providing continuous occupancy monitoring, unauthorised access alerts, and structural integrity tracking, all while preserving complete privacy.

Key Benefits

  • ✓ Continuous pool area monitoring without any cameras
  • ✓ Detects presence through walls, fences, and pool covers
  • ✓ Unauthorised access alerts sent instantly to your phone
  • ✓ Micro-movement detection for advanced safety scenarios
  • ✓ Long-term structural monitoring of pool surroundings

Technical Specifications

How We WorkChannel State Information (CSI) analysis across 2.4/5/6 GHz
HardwareCustom ESP32-S3 nodes with enterprise access points
DetectionOccupancy, movement patterns, micro-movements, structural shifts
ProcessingButterworth filters, PCA, Doppler shift analysis
AlertingReal-time via MQTT over TLS to Comms Hub
PrivacyNo optical data — pure RF signal analysis, anonymised processing
