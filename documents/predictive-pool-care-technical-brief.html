Public technical brief

How predictive pool care helps customers make easier decisions.

Palatial Pools uses public-safe seasonal signals, customer-approved readings and service context to suggest the next helpful step. The system is designed to reduce guesswork, not to replace qualified pool technicians.

Helpful by default

Recommendations favour practical next actions: test water, confirm fitment, top up common consumables or book a technician.

Private by design

Public pages use only aggregated, non-identifying signals. Customer records stay inside Palatial Link and staff systems.

Human verified

High-risk chemical, leak, electrical, warranty and installation decisions are reviewed by the Palatial team.

What the system considers

The public website uses broad signals that are safe to show: season, product category, service type, visible page intent and customer-entered tool values. Account-specific history is only shown after authentication inside Palatial Link.

What we do not publish

  • Customer addresses, names, invoices, photos, service notes or technician comments.
  • Supplier credentials, internal margins, private operating procedures or proprietary scoring details.
  • Live AI chat logs or free-form chatbot conversations on public pages.

OpenAI-class intelligence may be used only for bounded analysis workflows such as photo diagnostics or chemical interpretation. The public site does not run a live AI chatbot.
