How Much Does It Cost to Run a Pool Pump in Melbourne?

Real numbers, real Neptune pump data — and a free calculator to show your exact savings.

⏱ 6 min read
Home › Blog › Pool Pump Costs Melbourne

If you've ever looked at your electricity bill and wondered how much your pool pump is costing you, you're not alone. Pool pumps are one of the biggest energy consumers in an Australian home — and most Melbourne pool owners are still running old single-speed pumps that are far less efficient than modern alternatives.

In this article, we'll break down the real running costs using actual wattage data from the pumps we install every week, and show you exactly how much you could save by upgrading to a Neptune inverter pump.

The Real Cost of a Standard Single-Speed Pump

A typical Melbourne pool runs a single-speed pump for 8 hours per day. Here's what that actually costs at Victoria's average electricity rate of 32¢/kWh:

The most common pump in Melbourne homes is the 1.5hp single-speed — costing over $1,000 per year to run. That's before you factor in summer, when many owners run their pump 10–16 hours a day.

Which Pump Is Right for Your Pool?

As a general guide:

  • Up to 35,000L pool: Neptune X-Eco NPE550 (0.88hp)
  • 35,000–55,000L pool: Neptune X-Eco NPE750 (1.10hp) — most common Melbourne pool
  • 55,000–80,000L pool: Neptune X-Eco NPE1500 or X-Pro NPX1500
  • 80,000L+ or heated pools: Neptune X-Eco NPE2000 or X-Pro NPX2000
