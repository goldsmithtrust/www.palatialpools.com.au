How It Works

How Pool Area Shield Works

WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) technology measures how human bodies distort wireless signals. When someone enters the monitored zone, the distortion pattern changes — triggering an instant alert.

01

Sensors Installed

Small ESP32 or WiFi 6 devices are installed around your pool zone. They continuously broadcast and receive WiFi signals, mapping the normal signal pattern of an empty pool area.

02

Baseline Learned

Over 24–48 hours, the system learns the baseline signal fingerprint of your specific pool environment — accounting for wind, water movement, and nearby structures.

03

Presence Detected

When a human body enters the zone, it absorbs and reflects WiFi signals differently. The system detects this distortion in milliseconds and classifies it as a presence event.

04

Alert Sent Instantly

An SMS is sent to your registered phone number within seconds. You can configure quiet hours, multiple recipients, and zone-specific rules from the customer portal.

Installation

Professional Installation

Every Pool Area Shield system is installed by our trained technicians. No DIY required — we handle everything from hardware placement to system calibration.

01

Site Assessment

Our technician visits your property to map the pool zone, identify optimal sensor placement locations, and assess any environmental factors (water features, large trees, nearby WiFi interference) that may affect signal quality.

02

Hardware Installation

Sensor nodes are installed in weatherproof enclosures at strategic positions around the pool perimeter. Each node connects to your home WiFi network. Installation typically takes 2–3 hours.

03

Baseline Calibration

The system runs for 24–48 hours to learn the normal signal environment of your empty pool zone. This calibration period ensures accurate detection and minimises false alerts.

04

Alert Configuration & Handover

We configure your alert recipients, quiet hours, and zone sensitivity settings. You receive access to your personal monitoring dashboard and a walkthrough of all features.
