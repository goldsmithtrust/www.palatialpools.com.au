Methodology & Assumptions: Running costs use Melbourne climate averages — Summer ~20°C, Extended season ~15°C, Year-round ~12°C ambient. Electricity priced at the 2025 Victorian residential default offer. Gas priced at the average Melbourne retail rate. Heat pump COP values based on mid-range manufacturer specifications at each ambient temperature. Pool cover reduces heat loss by approximately 70%. Actual savings will vary with pool insulation, location, installation quality, and individual usage patterns. Solar heating excluded as running cost is near-zero (pump-only cost).
