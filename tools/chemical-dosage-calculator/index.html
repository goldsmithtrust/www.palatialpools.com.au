Methodology & Disclaimer: Dosages are calculated using industry-standard formulas for Australian pool conditions. Actual results may vary based on water temperature, bather load, sunlight exposure, and chemical product concentration. All figures are approximate — always retest 4–6 hours after adding chemicals. Never add more than one chemical at a time. Wait at least 4 hours between adjustments. For salt chlorinator pools, maintain salt at manufacturer-recommended levels (typically 3,000–5,000 ppm).
