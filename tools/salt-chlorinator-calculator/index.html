Pool Volume Minimum Cell Recommended Cell Heavy Use

Methodology & Assumptions: Chlorinator sizing is calculated using industry-standard rates of ~0.7 g/hr per 1,000 L for a baseline residential pool. Multipliers are applied for bather load (up to ×1.35 for heavy use), sun exposure (up to ×1.25 for full sun), pool type (indoor pools use ×0.75), and stabiliser level. Prices shown are indicative retail (inc. GST) as at 2026 and may vary by retailer. Cell warranty periods are from manufacturer specifications. Always consult a qualified pool professional for final sizing. Running a cell at 50–60% capacity (rather than 100%) can extend cell life by 30–50%.
