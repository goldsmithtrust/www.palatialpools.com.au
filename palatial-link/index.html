Service Dashboard Features

What It Helps Us Manage

These tools help our team manage day-to-day operations with precision and clarity.

Sales & Payments

Manage quotes, invoices, and payments in one place. Automated follow-ups and real-time financial tracking for every job.

Water Testing

Direct integration with water testing hardware. Results are automatically synced to customer profiles and previous service history.

Photo Diagnostics

Technicians upload photos of equipment and pool conditions. AI-assisted analysis helps identify wear and potential failures early.

Chemical Calculator

Calculate exact dosages for chlorine, pH adjustment, alkalinity, calcium hardness, cyanuric acid, and salt. Input pool volume and current readings, get precise grams or kilograms.

Volume Calculator

Calculate pool volume for rectangular, circular, oval, kidney, and freeform shapes. Returns litres, gallons, surface area, salt requirements, and pump turnover rate.

Label Printer

Create product labels with name, SKU, barcode, price, warnings, batch number, and expiry date. SVG preview with print-ready output for Brother QL-800.

Shared Records

Automatic synchronisation across contacts, jobs, pools, products, and quotes. Real-time data, no manual imports, no stale records.
