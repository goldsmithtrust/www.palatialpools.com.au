Filters & Media Toorak 3 photos

Media Change, Toorak

Your sand filter requires media changes in frequencies that depend on the type of media inside it. Sand needs to be changed every 5 years, whilst Zeolite should be changed every 5-7 years. Glass based media can last upwards of 10-15 years depending on the maintenance (backwashing) it has received.
